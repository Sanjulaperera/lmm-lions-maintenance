Good questions

Before you ask.

Are quotes really free?

Yes. We come out, measure up, talk through options and send a fixed written quote. No call-out fee and no obligation.

Can you sort out a shared fence with my neighbour?

We can. We regularly quote dividing fences and can give you an itemised quote to share with your neighbour so costs are split fairly.

Do I need a permit for a deck or retaining wall?

Some do, depending on height, location and your council. We will tell you on site if a permit or engineering is likely needed and help you through it.

What happens to the old fence and rubbish?

Removal and disposal of old fencing, soil and green waste can be included in your quote. We leave the site clean at the end of every day.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and job size. Give us a call and we will give you an honest start date — not just the one you want to hear.
